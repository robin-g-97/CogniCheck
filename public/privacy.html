Privacy

Privacy and data handling

CogniCheck is used with dashboard screenshots, requirements and business context. These inputs can be sensitive, so the workflow should be handled deliberately.

1

What may be uploaded

Users may upload dashboard screenshots, requirements notes and supporting context documents for analysis.

2

How AI is used

Uploaded content is sent to the configured AI provider to generate structured context, BI blueprints and concise CogniCheck analyses.

3

Analytics, inputs and outputs

If database analytics are enabled, CogniCheck may store page views, analysis counts, login email addresses, submitted analysis inputs and generated LLM outputs for product improvement and usage-pattern review.

4

Confidentiality

For workshops or pilots, sensitive dashboards and requirements should be shared only when appropriate for the organization.

5

Deletion requests

For questions or deletion requests, contact Robin at robin@cognicheck.tech.

Practical recommendation

For early pilots, use anonymized or representative dashboards where possible. For sensitive production reports, agree on scope and handling before the workshop starts.
